Toronto 2012

First International Left Hand Path Conference

Toronto, Ontario · Summer 2012 · In person

The opening weekend. Produced by Jeremy Crow as a gathering for the whole Left Hand Path rather than a single school, coven, or order. The aim was the same as the Luciferian Research Society’s public ethos: put people from different lineages in one room and let collaboration outrun grievance.

Jeremy Crow also gave a talk, as he did at every later gathering in this line.

Talks and panel

Confirmed lectures include Jeremy Crow, Ryan Murray, Rosmarinus Stehlik, and Laurie Pneumatikos. Salako Kalfou sat on the closing panel discussion. He is believed to have given a talk as well. The title is not recovered. Jeremy Crow missed most of that panel because he had already gone to the Cadillac Lounge to help set up the live music.

Light Bringers Art Show

Gallery 1313, Toronto. 25 July to 5 August 2012. Curator: Victoria Holmes. Producer: Jeremy Crow.

Artists: SINS, Alejandro Freeland, Lucero Miltxorena, Calla Donofrio, David Carlton, Rosmarinus Stehlik, Tasha Menary, Xris Kukiel, Fox Xoft, James Alexander Minett, Jennifer Humphries, John Nobrega, Juan Ramirez, Jubal Brown, Orlee Andromedae (now Orlee Stewart).

Alejandro Freeland posted a gallery walk-through on 18 August 2012: Light Bringers Art Show on Vimeo.

The full artist list also lives on the art page.

Live music at the Cadillac Lounge

The music night attached to the conference. Confirmed acts: The Darren White; Pleasure The Priestess; spoken word by Scott Martin. One further band name is not yet recovered.

Black Magick Burlesque and Cabaret

A live theatrical evening produced by Jeremy Crow on 3 August 2012 at The Great Hall.

  • Ms. Chaos Divine, act titled Iron Feather, to Skrillex, "Breakn' A Sweat." Recording: YouTube
  • Dr. Robert Ing, mentalist and magician
  • Dew Lily (Willard Gillard), burlesque
  • Penny Whistleton, burlesque
  • Ilana Mitzy Kruger, performing as Miss Mitzy Cream, burlesque

Other acts from that night are not yet named here.

What survives

Founder’s account; recovered site copy; the Light Bringers artist list and dates; Freeland’s Vimeo record; the Chaos Divine clip; names from the Cadillac Lounge and the cabaret. Talk titles from the lecture hall are still thin.

What does not

No public multi-camera record of the lectures comparable to 2021. One Cadillac Lounge band name, further cabaret names, and Salako Kalfou’s talk title remain open.
