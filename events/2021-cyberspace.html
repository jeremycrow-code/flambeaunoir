Cyberspace 2021

The Waking Dream

29 to 31 January 2021 · Virtual · Free to the public

Travel was still constrained. The conference moved online and was offered without a ticket price. Attendees sent tips; those were divided equally among presenters after the weekend. A 3D virtual Lightbearers art show ran alongside, curated by Art Director Răzvan Anton with artist Jaymie Dylan.
