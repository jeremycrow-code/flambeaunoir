Portland 2018

Darkness Indivisible

Portland, Oregon · 27 to 29 April 2018 · Last in-person weekend

Friday opened with The Descent of Ishtar, an initiation rite for Ziggurat of Enki. Saturday and Sunday were talks. The weekend closed with Darkness Audible, music and performance, at the Lovecraft Bar on Walpurgisnacht Eve, under a full moon in Scorpio.

The working address was given only to verified ticket holders. It stays unpublished here.

Presenters named in public notices

Among those who appeared: Paul Fredric, Peter Grey, Venus Satanas, Michael W. Ford, Coyote, Sean Donahue, Jeremy Crow (on Death). The public Facebook event carried a fuller artist and vendor list. Names announced who did not present are omitted here.
