The Black Torch

A torch exists to carry flame. In the Left Hand Path that flame is often called the Black Flame: the perturbing spark that gives rise to form. Flambeau Noir, French for black torch, was a vessel for that current: a weekend in which people from different lineages could share a room without converting one another.

The conference is not currently scheduled. This site is the public record of the weekends that already happened, and the notice board if the fire is lit again.
